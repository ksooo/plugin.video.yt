v1.1.1
- With Kodi 23, a video marked unwatched or whose resume point was reset now leaves the History folder right away
- With Kodi 23, a video watched to the end in Kodi now stays in your YouTube watch history

v1.1.0
- New folder "Live" with the live streams YouTube features now, the upcoming ones and those just ended
- New folder "All subscriptions" with the newest videos of all subscribed channels together
- New folder "Likes" with the videos you liked
- Like a video or remove your like from its context menu
- A video's plot now starts with its channel and its numbers of views and likes
- Playing a live stream that has not started yet tells when it starts
- With Kodi 23, marking a video watched or unwatched and resetting its resume point also reach your YouTube watch history
- Syncing the playback state with YouTube is now off by default; it can be switched on in the playback settings
- After signing in with another account, the previous account's recommendations no longer show up
