v1.3.2
- Video lists open faster, "All subscriptions" about twice as fast

v1.3.1
- Playing a video no longer leaves error messages in the Kodi log

v1.3.0
- Requires Kodi 22 (Piers) or newer
- Channels and "All subscriptions" now start sorted by date, newest first; the subscriptions and the trending categories start sorted by name
- Video titles starting with an emoji are no longer shortened; Kodi 22 shows such titles blank

v1.2.1
- With Kodi 23, a video resumed and stopped again within its first three minutes no longer loses where it was stopped

v1.2.0
- Where Kodi adjusts the display's refresh rate to a video, it now does so before playback starts instead of a few seconds into it
- Audio tracks YouTube dubbed automatically are named so in the audio stream dialogue; a new setting stops offering them
- The original audio track of a video YouTube dubbed automatically is now recognised as such
- In a video's context menu, visiting its channel and subscribing or unsubscribing now share a submenu named after the channel
- The playback settings are grouped into general, video and audio
- "All subscriptions" no longer comes up empty at times
- When the daily YouTube quota is used up, the message now tells when it is available again

v1.1.1
- With Kodi 23, a video marked unwatched or whose resume point was reset now leaves the History folder right away
- With Kodi 23, a video watched to the end in Kodi now stays in your YouTube watch history

v1.1.0
- New folder "Live" with the live streams YouTube features now, the upcoming ones and those just ended
- New folder "All subscriptions" with the newest videos of all subscribed channels together
- New folder "Likes" with the videos you liked
- Like a video or remove your like from its context menu
- A video's plot now starts with its channel and its numbers of views and likes
- Playing a live stream that has not started yet tells when it starts
- With Kodi 23, marking a video watched or unwatched and resetting its resume point also reach your YouTube watch history
- Syncing the playback state with YouTube is now off by default; it can be switched on in the playback settings
- After signing in with another account, the previous account's recommendations no longer show up

v1.0.0
- Initial release
